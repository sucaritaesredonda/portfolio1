100 Jahre Kraftwerk Partenstein: Cyanotopia collage

This project was inspired by a visit to the Partenstein Kraftwerk hydroelectric plant. Using digital and analogue cyanotype techniques, it is a collage that plays with the strong contrast between nature and industry present in that landscape.
